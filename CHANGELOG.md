# Changes for staff

## v1.0.0

- First live version at app.gildedgold.org.

## v1.0.2

- The side menu now shows the company name under "Gilded".

## v1.1.0

- Busy days: a director can switch off the note-by-note drawer rule for a branch (Company settings > Cash and allowance). There the teller enters the notes actually handed out, which must add up to the cash amount. The money in the drawer is always enforced.
- New "Customer" tab in the tools panel (round button, bottom right): on the New transaction page, tap the button after entering a customer and it opens on that customer's latest purchases (date, time, top, down, carat, amount). If the customer does not exist, or has no purchases, it says so.
- New "Closing" tab in the tools panel (round button): at any time, see what your closing account would be if you closed now: cash (bundles and loose), debtors, goods in stock (tap to see each purchase), what is owed to customers, and the closing balance. It only shows; nothing is closed from it.

## v1.2.1

- Nothing visible for staff (the website no longer intercepts the desktop installer download address).

## v1.2.2

- Phone: the bottom menu is cleaner (rounded highlight on the current tab, shorter labels "History" and "Drawer", a raised New button).
- Drawer: separate green "Cash in" and red "Cash out" buttons; the form now opens on the one you pressed, with a coloured total and a clear button ("Add cash" / "Take cash out").
- Calculators: the Classic | Focus switch (big inputs, big result, number pad on touch screens) now works on the calculators too, same choice as New transaction.
- All pop-up windows with a navy header no longer have a white frame around the header.

## v1.3.0

- Managers now get a notification when the drawer ran short at a branch hub: an entry saved while a computer could not reach the hub, which the drawer had no notes for.

## v1.4.0

- Nothing visible for staff yet (the server is ready for opening and closing the day with no internet).

## v1.5.0

- You can now open and close the day with no internet. The day opens or closes on the computer at once and is sent when the connection is back, in order. The page tells you when it is using this computer's own books.
- If the server's books turn out different when an offline count arrives, the count is kept and the manager is told.

## v1.6.0

- Branches with several computers: opening and closing the day with no internet now shows on every computer, and only one computer can open or close a day.
- Fixed: on the second day of using a branch hub, the drawer on the computers could show too little (yesterday's payouts were taken off again)

## v1.7.0

- New: search everything from anywhere. Press Ctrl+K or tap the search button and type a name, a phone number, a receipt number or an amount. It also finds pages ("close the day"). On a customer, Shift+Enter (or the + button) starts a new purchase for them.

## v1.8.0

- New page "Gold market": the live world gold price, our own price over time, what we bought, and the price changes. Also a "Market" tab in the tools panel.
- The world chart is for watching only. It is never the price we pay.

## v0.3.0 (desktop app)

- Updated desktop functionality since v1.2.1:
  - Added offline opening and closing of the day, with automatic syncing when the connection returns.
  - Shared offline opening and closing status across computers at a branch, while ensuring only one computer can perform each action.
  - Added manager notifications for offline drawer shortages and mismatched offline counts.
  - Added global search for customers, phone numbers, receipts, amounts, and pages (Ctrl+K).
  - Added quick access to a customer's latest purchases and the ability to start a new purchase for that customer.
  - Added the Gold market page and Market tools tab with world gold prices, purchase history, and price changes.
  - Improved phone navigation, drawer cash-in/cash-out controls, calculator layouts, and pop-up window styling.

## v1.9.0

- A manager or director can cancel a purchase saved by mistake. The books are reversed, the cash returns to today's drawer, and the purchase stays listed as cancelled.

## v1.10.0

- Gold transfers record the scale reading against the books, coloured by how far apart they are; a large gap notifies reviewers.

## v1.11.0

- The dashboard shows each branch's purchases over time as lines (cedis, weight or count, 7 days to 12 months); tellers see their own branch. Dates across the app now show the weekday.

## v0.4.0 (desktop app)

- Updated desktop functionality since v0.3.0 (includes web v1.9.0 to v1.11.0):
  - A manager or director can cancel a purchase saved by mistake: the books are reversed, the cash returns to today's drawer, and the purchase stays listed as cancelled.
  - Gold transfers show totals while choosing and record the scale reading against the books, coloured by how far apart they are; a large gap notifies reviewers.
  - The dashboard shows each branch's purchases over time as lines (cedis, weight or count, 7 days to 12 months); tellers see their own branch.
  - Dates across the app now show the weekday.

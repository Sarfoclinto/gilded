# Changes for staff

## v1.0.0
- First live version at app.gildedgold.org.

## v1.0.2
- The side menu now shows the company name under "Gilded".

## v1.1.0
- Busy days: a director can switch off the note-by-note drawer rule for a branch (Company settings > Cash and allowance). There the teller enters the notes actually handed out, which must add up to the cash amount. The money in the drawer is always enforced.
- New "Customer" tab in the tools panel (round button, bottom right): on the New transaction page, tap the button after entering a customer and it opens on that customer's latest purchases (date, time, top, down, carat, amount). If the customer does not exist, or has no purchases, it says so.
- New "Closing" tab in the tools panel (round button): at any time, see what your closing account would be if you closed now: cash (bundles and loose), debtors, goods in stock (tap to see each purchase), what is owed to customers, and the closing balance. It only shows; nothing is closed from it.

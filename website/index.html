How a sale works

Most sales take a few minutes. You can stop at any step.

  1. Bring your gold

    Dust, nuggets, bars or jewellery. Come to any branch during opening hours.

  2. We weigh it twice, with you watching

    Once in air and once in water. From the two readings we work out the density, and from that the karat and purity.

  3. We price it at the day's rate

    The price follows the day's reference rate for gold. You see the weights, the karat and the amount before you agree.

  4. You are paid on the spot

    In cash or by mobile money, with a printed receipt that lists every figure.

Nothing hidden

Both readings, the karat and the price are on the screen in front of you. Ask us to weigh again at any time.

A receipt every time

Every sale is recorded and every customer gets a receipt. If you have a question later, we can look it up.

The same at every branch

Every branch uses the same scales, the same method and the same day's rate.

## Decisions recorded

- **Goods received by a branch through a transfer stay on that branch's books as stock** until a later sale or refining step (B5, option A).
- **Failed mobile-money payment settled in cash**: the purchase branch pays the notes from its own drawer (cash-out). No notes are added to the drawer of the branch that was to send the money; instead the amount appears against that branch in the between-branches position (`mobile_money_failed_covered_by_cash`), as money it still has to hand over. Nothing is netted automatically; management settles it.
- **Customer editing** is the permission `customers:manage` (default manager and director; a teller only by personal grant).

## Requested additions (not yet built)

1. **Calculator to purchase.** Pass the calculator result to the new-purchase screen through router state (items, price, result) and autofill it; the user then adds customer and payout. Nothing is saved until they confirm the purchase, so nothing half-finished is stored.
2. **Utility panel.** One button in the top bar (floating on mobile) opens the calculator or the nominations calculator beside the current page. Desktop: a split view the user can resize and close, with its inputs kept while they move between pages. Mobile: a bottom sheet that covers about 70% of the screen, so the page behind stays in place. The panel never saves anything by itself.
3. **Closing screen, two views.** "Continuous" (current behaviour, from the beginning to that day) and "This day only": debtors with amounts and their sum as at that time, number of bundles per denomination and their value, loose notes per denomination, total cash, value of goods and total debts.

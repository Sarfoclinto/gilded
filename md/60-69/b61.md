# B61: closing-day photo + loan security photo (v1.13.0)

**No photo is ever required.** Missing ones are only flagged for reviewers.

## What changed
- **Close the day**: under "2 · Gold in the branch", optional "Photo of the scaled gold". Works offline: queued on the phone, sent when online (a 404 for a day that has not synced yet is treated as "wait", up to 3 days).
- **Day history**: amber chip "No closing photo" on closed days; the photo can be added or viewed late in the day detail.
- **Give a loan is now a page** (`/debts/new-loan`, DEBTS_MANAGE), not a modal. `LoanModal.tsx` deleted. Under "Security" an optional photo of the gold on the scale; it is held in memory and queued against the held gold once the loan is saved.
- **Held gold** page: photo on each card (late photo allowed; read-only once returned/converted) and amber "No photo" chip.
- Backend: migration 0061 (`attachment_visible`, `add_attachment` for `day`/`collateral`, `photo_count`, `day_json_v4` wrapped with `photos`, `collateral_json_v2` wrapped with `photos`), permissions DaysManage/DaysRead and DebtsManage/DebtsRead. openapi updated.

## Tested
DB tests 54 files pass; web `tsc -b` clean, vitest 386 pass.
## NOT tested
C# (no dotnet here): AttachmentsService edits + new unit test. Not browser-checked: the loan page and the close-day photo with a real camera.

## How to test
1. Debts → Give a loan → tick security, add a photo, give the loan → Held gold shows the photo.
2. Give a loan with security and no photo → "No photo" chip; add one from the card.
3. Close the day with a photo, also with the network off, then back on.
4. Day history → closed day without photo shows the chip.

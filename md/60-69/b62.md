# B62: Photos library, purchase photos, customer ID photos (v1.14.0)

No photo is ever required. Nothing here blocks a purchase.

## What changed

- **Photos page** (menu, under Held gold; needs the new permission `attachments:library`): every picture from the branches you can see, newest first, as a grid. Filters: kind (transfers, closing the day, purchases, held gold, customer IDs), branch, dates, and a search box (name, receipt or transfer number). Each tile links to its record; tap to open the viewer (swipe, zoom, remove with a reason). Removed pictures leave the page; the record is kept.
- **New purchase**: optional "The gold on the scale" and "The customer's ID" under the items. They go to the photo queue when you save, so they work offline.
- **Purchase page**: a Photos section (add or view later). The ID picture shows there only to people with `attachments:ids`.
- **Customer page**: "ID pictures" (same permission).
- **IDs are private**: new permission `attachments:ids` (given to managers and directors; change it on the Roles page). Without it IDs are not listed, not in the library, not served. Opening one writes `attachment.id_viewed` to the Audit trail (once per person per picture per 10 minutes).
- Backend: migration 0062 (new places `transaction`/`customer`, `attachment_open`, `photo_library`, `remove_attachment` with ID awareness), `GET /attachments/library`, two permissions, openapi updated, role editor lists both permissions.

## Tested

DB: 55 files pass (new 055). Web: tsc clean, vitest 386 pass; Photos page checked in a browser with a stubbed server (grid, filter request, viewer, no console errors; the fake picture bytes were not decodable, so I did not see real thumbnails).

## NOT tested

C# (no dotnet here), including the new unit tests. The purchase and customer-page photo sections were only type-checked; ID view logging only through SQL.

## How to test

1. Sign in as a manager: Photos in the menu. Make a transfer, a closing photo and a purchase with photos: they appear, newest first; filter and search them.
2. As a teller: no Photos menu item; the new purchase form still offers both photo boxes; the purchase page shows the gold photo but no ID box.
3. As a manager open a customer's ID, then the Audit trail: a line for it (only one if you reopen it within 10 minutes).
4. Offline: take both photos, save the purchase, reconnect; they arrive.

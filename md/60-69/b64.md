# B64: login, My settings, Company settings and Change password, redesigned (v1.15.1, web only)

No backend, database or setting changes. Nothing to install at night except the web deploy.

## What changed
- **Login**: the placeholder `G` box is replaced by the real crown-and-GG mark (the same file the header, side menu and browser icon already use). Desktop: a navy panel with the mark inside a slowly turning coin-edge ring (stops for people who asked their device for less motion), the headline, and the form beside it. Phone: the panel becomes a short header and the form rises over it. Added an Online/Offline pill; larger inputs, gold focus, a plain "Signing in…" state. Same behaviour: same fields, offline sign-in, errors.
- **My settings**: a navy profile banner (large photo with a gold ring, name, role and branches, Change my password); settings grouped into five cards with icons (Receipts, New purchases, Notifications, Screen, This device); a menu of sections beside the page on desktop that highlights where you are, and a row of chips on a phone; switches stay on the line, choices drop under the text on a phone; Forget this device and Install stated more clearly. Same settings, same behaviour, still kept on this device.
- New shared pieces: `BrandMark`, `Wordmark`, `CoinEdge` (`shared/ui/BrandMark.tsx`); `ProfilePhoto` has an `onDark` look.

- **Company settings**: same banner as My settings; the groups (Working offline, Paying customers, Receipts, Business details, Cash and allowance, Photos, Gold market) are a menu beside the page on desktop and chips on a phone, each with an icon and an amber dot while it has unsaved changes; the fields sit in one card per group; the sample receipt stays beside Receipts and Business details. The unsaved-changes bar now floats at the bottom with Discard and Save changes. Still nothing is saved until Save; the groups still come from `settings/model.ts` (a new group gets a cog icon until one is added to `ICONS`). The `?tab=` link still works.
- **Change password**: the banner with a shield, a form card with larger boxes, a four-step strength bar with its word, the three rules as chips that turn green, and a "Keep it yours" tips card (below the form on a phone). Same rules, same messages, same forced-change flow.
- `settings/FieldRow.tsx`, `DesktopRows.tsx` and `BranchComputers.tsx` rows were restyled to sit inside the new cards (a switch keeps its line on a phone).

## Left as it was
The printed receipt still has a plain `G` in a circle (`features/receipt/Receipt.tsx`). The mark is gold, which does not print well on a black-and-white receipt printer; it needs a one-colour version of the mark. Tell me if you want it.

## Tested
tsc clean, vitest 386 pass. All four pages looked at in a browser at phone width and desktop width with sample data (no console errors). Not looked at: the desktop-app-only rows (hub and branch computers), which only show in the installed app. Not tried: a real phone, the tablet width, the installed desktop app, and dark mode (the app has none).

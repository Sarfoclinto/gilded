# B66: share a private picture with a colleague (v1.17.0)

Builds on B65 (My pictures). Needs the database migration (0065), the API deploy and the web deploy. Branch is based on `feat-personal-pictures`, so B65 must be applied first.

## What people get
- On **My pictures**, select one or more pictures and tap **Share**: choose one colleague and confirm. The colleague gets a notification ("Ama shared 2 pictures with you") that opens the new **Shared with me** tab.
- The dialog says plainly what happens: they can look at the pictures and save a copy, they cannot share them on, you can stop sharing any time and their access ends at once (a copy already saved cannot be taken back), and the company's activity log records that you shared with this person (not the pictures, not when they are looked at).
- A small people badge on a thumbnail shows it is shared; the full-screen view says "Shared with Kojo". **Stop sharing** appears in the selection bar when any selected picture is shared.
- **Shared with me** tab (with a count): pictures grouped by who shared them, opened in the same viewer (pinch, swipe, save/share). The colleague can **Remove from my list**; the owner is not told and can still stop sharing.
- Deleting a picture ends every share of it.

## Rules (all enforced by the database)
- Only the owner can share, stop, caption or delete. A colleague cannot change a shared picture, cannot share it on, and the picture does not count against their space.
- The director or anyone else with every photo permission still sees nothing: a share is only readable by its owner and its recipient.
- Only sharing and stopping are logged (action `personal.shared` / `personal.unshared`, with the colleague and a count). Taking, viewing, deleting and hiding write nothing.
- Colleagues you can pick from: every active person in the company except yourself.

## How to test
1. As Ama, take two pictures, open My pictures, Select both, Share, choose Kojo.
2. As Kojo: a notification arrives; tap it, the Shared with me tab shows Ama's two pictures; open one.
3. As Ama: a people badge is on both; Select one, Stop sharing. As Kojo, refresh: one is gone.
4. As Ama delete the other: Kojo's tab empties. As the director: nothing of either is visible anywhere (Photos page included); Activity log shows "shared" and "stopped sharing" lines only.

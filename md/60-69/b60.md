# B60 — Photos: transfers (hand-over and receipt) and profile pictures

Builds on the B60a try-out (v1.11.1). The camera, the shrinking and the background sending are the same ones you tested; this makes them real.

## What staff get
- **Transfers.** A "Photos" card on every transfer: **At handover** (sending branch) and **At receipt** (receiving branch).
  - **Hand to the collector** and **Receive** windows now have an optional photo box ("Photo of the gold on the scale", "Photo of what arrived"). Tap **Add photo**: the in-app camera opens and stays open for several shots; the library button picks many at once; "Use the phone's own camera app…" is the fallback. Same sheet you tried.
  - **Nothing is ever required or blocked.** If a transfer was handed over or received with no photo, the card shows a plain yellow line: *"No photo was attached at handover. Photos are not required; this only lets the reviewer know there is none."* Add one late and the line disappears.
  - Every photo is stamped on the picture itself: branch · transfer reference / day, date and time with weekday, person. Thumbnails open a full-screen viewer (swipe, pinch, double-tap, arrow keys).
  - A photo taken with no signal is saved on the phone and sent when the connection returns, even after the app is closed. A small ring shows it is on its way.
  - **Remove**: the person who took it, or anyone with the new permission **"Take other people's photos off the screen"** (`attachments:remove`, given to managers and directors; any custom role can have it). The picture is not erased: the file and a record of who removed it and why are kept, and the transfer's timeline says "Photo added / Photo removed".
- **Profile picture.** My settings → **Add a photo / Change photo / Remove**. It shows in the top bar beside your name, and everyone in the company sees it (initials on gold when there is none).
- Phones sign in as a person; a photo waiting on a shared phone is only sent when its owner is signed in.

## Where the photos live
On the server, in a Docker volume called `photos` (`/data/photos` in the API container): one file per picture plus a small preview, sorted by company and year. The database holds the facts (what it is attached to, who, when, size, source, whether stamped, and the file's SHA-256). Nobody can fetch a file without signing in; a transfer's photos can only be seen by people at the sending or receiving branch, profile pictures by the whole company.
- `scripts/backup.sh` now also saves the photos next to the database copy (`gilded-…-photos.tar.gz`, newest 14 kept). `scripts/restore.sh backups/gilded-X.sql.gz [backups/gilded-X-photos.tar.gz]` puts both back. **Photos can grow large over the years: keep an eye on the disk, and copy the backups off the server.**

## Decisions to check
- No photo is required anywhere; a missing one is only flagged on the transfer page (not yet in the reviewers' inbox or the dashboard; that is B63).
- A removed photo is hidden, never deleted from the disk (evidence). Say if you want an automatic clean-up after some years.
- Only JPEG photos up to 8 MB are accepted (the app shrinks everything to ~1600 px / 300 KB before sending, HEIC included).
- A profile picture can only be changed by its owner (a manager cannot set someone else's).
- Stamp is always on for transfer photos (it was a switch in the lab). Say if tellers should be able to turn it off.

## How to test by hand
1. Night install (below). Run `docker compose exec api ls -ld /data/photos` : it should belong to `app`.
2. Open a draft transfer on a phone, set the collector, **Hand to the collector**: take two photos in the box, finish the hand-over. On the transfer page the **At handover** card shows them (no yellow line).
3. Hand over another one **without** a photo: the yellow "No photo was attached…" line shows; add one afterwards: the line goes.
4. Switch the phone to aeroplane mode, take a photo: it shows the waiting clock; turn the network back on: it uploads by itself.
5. At the receiving branch (other phone/login): the same transfer shows the handover photos and an **At receipt** card; add photos while receiving.
6. Remove a photo (your own); as a manager remove someone else's; a teller must not see the bin on others' photos.
7. My settings → Add a photo; your picture appears in the top bar. Log in as someone else: your picture shows next to your name only for you, and the other person's initials/picture for them.
8. `./scripts/backup.sh` creates two files; `ls backups`.

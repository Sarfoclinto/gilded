# B65: My pictures, a private camera for every user (v1.16.0)

Phase 1 of the approved design (`docs/personal-pictures-design.md`): take, keep, caption, search and delete. Sharing with a colleague is B66.
Needs the database migration (0064) and the API deploy as well as the web deploy.

## What people get
- A **camera button on every screen**: a round icon in the top bar on a computer; on phones and tablets a small navy-and-gold round button just above the Tools button. Tap it, take as many pictures as you like (the library is allowed too), tap Done: "Saved to My pictures". No customer, no purchase, no stamp.
- Pictures are saved on the device first and sent in the background, so it works with no connection and sends when the signal returns (same queue as company photos, separate records).
- **My pictures** page (account menu > My pictures, or search "my pictures"): navy banner saying only you can see these, how much space you have used, search by caption, pictures grouped by day, tap to open full size (pinch, swipe, share sheet), **Select** to delete one or many for good or to give one picture a caption. "On their way" shows pictures still being sent, with Try again for any that failed. A warning appears when you are near your limit.
- **Company settings > My pictures**: switch on/off, pictures per person (300), space per person (300 MB), days a leaver's pictures are kept (90). Off hides the buttons and stops new pictures; it deletes nothing and shows nothing to anyone.

## How it stays private
- Separate table and folder from the company's photos (`gold.personal_pictures`, files under `tenant/p/owner/`). The Photos library, missing-photo list, ID logging and removal tools cannot reach them.
- The database checks the signed-in person on every read and write (row-level security). No role or permission opens them: a director sees an empty list and gets "not found" for a guessed id. Tested.
- Served with `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. Taking, viewing and deleting write no audit line.
- Delete is real: the row goes, then the server removes the files.
- People who leave: when an account is switched off its pictures are unreachable to everyone and are deleted after the set days (hourly clean-up job). Switching the account back on before then restores them to that person only.
- Honest limit: the files are on your server, so someone with root access to the machine could technically reach them. Encryption at rest is a later step.

## How to test
1. Sign in as a teller, tap the round camera button, take two pictures, Done. They appear under My pictures (account menu).
2. Sign in as the director on another device: My pictures is empty. Nothing of the teller's shows anywhere, including the Photos page.
3. Select a picture, add a caption, search for it, delete it.
4. Company settings > My pictures: set "Pictures per person" to 2, try a third picture (refused with a clear message); switch off and the buttons disappear.
5. Take a picture with the phone in flight mode: it shows "On their way", then arrives when the signal is back.

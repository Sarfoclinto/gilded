# B60a — Photos spike: try the camera on your real phones and computers

This is the "step 0" of docs/PHOTOS-DESIGN.md. **It is a try-out page, not the final feature.** Nothing is sent to the server and no database change is needed. It exists so you can feel the camera, the shrinking and the background sending on the actual iPhones, Androids, tablets and webcams, next to WhatsApp, before anything is built on top of it.

## Decisions taken from your answers (now built into the plan)
1. Photos are stored in a **volume on the Hetzner server** (not built yet; this spike has no server part).
2. **No photo is ever required and none blocks work.** At transfer hand-over and day close, a missing photo is **flagged for the reviewer** ("no photo attached"), nothing more.
3. **Phones first, mostly iPhones**, a few Androids, then tablets and computers with built-in or external webcams. This is why the spike has two ways to take a picture (below), so we can see which one is best on an iPhone.

## What is on the page (`/photos-lab`, not in the menu; open it by typing the address)
- **"Add photos (app camera)":** a full-screen camera inside Gilded. Stays open for many shots; a strip of thumbnails builds at the bottom; tap a thumbnail to look at it or remove it; **Done (3)** hands them all back. Torch button where the phone allows it. If the device has two cameras there is a switch button; with three or more (a computer with a webcam and a capture card, say) there is a picker, and your choice is remembered. Space bar takes a shot on a computer.
- **"Add photos (phone camera app)":** uses the phone's own camera app (one picture per tap). This is usually the most dependable on iPhones and gives the phone's best picture quality.
- **Choose from the library** (the picture icon in both): several at once. On a computer you can also **paste (Ctrl+V)** or **drop files** on the page.
- **Shrinking on the device:** the picture is turned upright (the iPhone rotation flag is honoured), reduced to at most 1600 px on the long side and about 300 to 400 KB, a small thumbnail is made, and a fingerprint (SHA-256) is computed for duplicate detection later. The page shows how many milliseconds that took and "1.8 MB → 366 KB".
- **Optional stamp burned into the picture:** branch, reference, weekday + date + time, person.
- **Background sending (simulated):** each thumbnail shows a progress ring, a tick when sent, a "try again" if it failed. You choose the pretend speed (good Wi-Fi, mobile data, weak signal), can pretend there is **no connection**, and can make **every third send fail**. Pictures are kept on the device (IndexedDB), survive closing the page, and send when possible. At most two go at once; a lost connection is not counted as a failure; real failures retry with growing waits and then park for a manual "try again".
- **Viewer:** full screen, swipe or arrow keys between pictures, pinch / double-tap / mouse-wheel to zoom, size and details underneath.
- **"This device" box:** whether the page is secure, whether the camera API exists, whether it is installed as an app, and whether pictures survive a restart. **Please send me this box (and your phone model) if anything misbehaves.**

## What to test, and what to compare with WhatsApp
Do each on an **iPhone in Safari**, an **iPhone with Gilded installed to the home screen**, an **Android**, a **tablet**, and a **computer with a webcam**.
| Check | Good looks like |
|---|---|
| Open the app camera | live picture within about a second; the permission question appears once, not every time |
| Take 3 shots quickly | thumbnails appear at once; no freeze |
| Done | back on the page immediately; pictures show a spinner ring then a tick |
| Phone camera app button | opens the normal camera; the picture comes back rotated correctly |
| Library, pick 5 at once | all 5 arrive; HEIC pictures from an iPhone work (if one says it cannot be read, tell me) |
| Pretend no connection, add 2, reload the page | the 2 are still there, send when the pretend-offline box is cleared |
| Weak signal | you can keep adding while earlier ones are still sending |
| Torch (Android) | the lightning button appears and lights (iPhone Safari does not allow this: the button is hidden, which is expected) |
| Stamp | readable on the picture, at full size in the viewer |
| Webcam on a computer | the camera picker lists the external webcam; the choice is remembered |
| Lock the phone while sending | on an iPhone sending pauses and resumes when you come back; tell me if pictures are lost |

## Tested here
- Unit tests (10 new, 384 in all): shrink rules, stamp text with weekday, sizes, waits, queue (saves first, waits offline, a lost connection is not a failed try, refusals retry then park and "try again" revives, picks up what a closed app left, never more than two at once, drops the big file after sending). **These found a real bug in my first queue, where a picture could be sent twice; fixed before delivery.**
- In a test browser with a simulated camera and real files: 3 quick shots, Done, background sending; a 4032×3024 iPhone-style JPEG with the rotation flag came out upright (1200×1600) as 366 KB from 1.8 MB in 144 ms; pictures chosen while pretending to be offline were still there after a reload and then sent.
## NOT tested here (this is what you are for)
- **Any real camera, any real phone**, iPhone behaviour (permissions in an installed app, HEIC, locking the phone), the torch, tap-to-focus (not built), external webcams, speed on a mid-range Android, dark mode.
- Sending to the real server (does not exist yet; B60 builds it).

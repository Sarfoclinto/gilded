# B68: Workings phase 2: share a sheet, copy a result, a Tools-panel tab, pinch-zoom (v1.18.1)

Needs the database migration (0068), the API deploy and the web deploy. Based on `feat-workings` (B67), so apply B67 first.

## What people get
- **Copy a result.** Select a note with a figure or a result: the bar shows its value and a **Copy** button. It copies the exact figure (for example `15004`, no separators or unit) so it pastes cleanly into an amount box, and says "Copied 15004. Paste it where you need it."
- **Share a sheet.** On your own sheet, the people button opens Share: pick one colleague. The dialog lists who it is shared with, each with Stop sharing, and says plainly what sharing means. The colleague gets a notification that opens Workings > Shared with me.
- **Shared with me** tab on the Workings page (with a count): each sheet with who shared it and when it last changed. "Remove from my list" hides one; the owner is not told.
- **A shared sheet is read only.** A chip says "Shared by Kojo · read only". The colleague can scroll, zoom, select, see the dependency lines and Copy figures, but cannot write, move, delete or rename. It always shows the owner's latest version and looks again every 20 seconds while open. **Save a copy** makes it the colleague's own editable sheet, named "(copy)".
- Your own list says who each sheet is shared with.
- **Tools panel: a new "Sheet" tab** (when Workings is on). Pick one of your sheets, or New, and it opens beside whatever page you are on, for example a purchase. Select a result, Copy, paste into the purchase form. The sheet stays open when you switch Tools tabs.
- **Pinch-zoom** on phones and tablets: two fingers zoom around the point between them (50% to 200%). On a laptop, a trackpad pinch (Ctrl + scroll) does the same. Zoom stops the browser zooming the whole page. The + and − buttons still step through the usual sizes.

## Rules (enforced by the database)
- Only the owner can share, stop, rename, change or delete. A colleague cannot save over a shared sheet, rename it, delete it or share it on.
- The director sees nothing of either person's sheets.
- Only "shared" and "stopped sharing" are logged (`working.shared` / `working.unshared`, with the colleague), never the contents or when it is opened.
- Deleting a sheet ends its shares.

# B67: Workings, phase 1: a freeform scratch sheet for rough calculations (v1.18.0)

Your four answers were built in: tablet and computer first, gold helpers in phase 1, private like My pictures, no pen. Needs the database migration (0067), the API deploy and the web deploy. Based on `feat-loan-existing-debt` (B59), so apply B65, B66 and B59 first.

## What people get
- **Workings** in the menu (under Calculator), search "workings", "scratch" or "rough".
- **The list**: your sheets, newest first, with name search, rename, duplicate and delete. A sheet still waiting to reach the server says "on this device".
- **A sheet**: a big dotted page.
  - Tap or click an empty spot to write a note, then Enter. `Weight 12.4 g` becomes a labelled figure; `rent` is just a note.
  - Start with `=` for a result: `= Weight × Price`, or name it: `Total = Weight × Price`. While writing a result, tap another note and its name goes in for you.
  - Maths: + − × ÷ ^ ( ), `sum`, `avg`, `min`, `max`, `round(x, 2)`, `abs`, and percentages (`Fee 5%` counts as 0.05).
  - Gold helpers: `purity(18)` gives 0.75, `pure(Weight, Karat)` gives pure grams, and `price` is today's price per gram for your branch (shown in the toolbar). A note you name "Price" takes priority.
  - Units are light: g, kg, oz, GHS, %, karat. Grams times a price gives GHS. Adding grams to cedis is refused, in words.
  - Results update as soon as any figure changes. Select a result to see dashed gold lines to the notes it uses.
  - Drag notes to move them. Drag on empty space (or use the select button) to pick several: a bar shows sum, average, min and max, and **Total note** writes `Total = sum(...)` under them. You can also Duplicate or Delete them.
  - Undo (Ctrl+Z), Delete key, Ctrl+D duplicates. Zoom from 50% to 200%, Print, and a ? with the cheat sheet.
  - Errors are sentences: "There is no note called "Weight"", "Two notes are called "Weight"…", "This goes round in a circle…", "Cannot divide by zero".
- **Saving**: every change goes to the device at once and to the server a moment later ("Saved on this device", then "Saved"). With no connection it keeps working and sends when the connection returns. If the same sheet was changed on another device, the server keeps its version and your version is kept as a second sheet, "<name> (other device)", so nothing is lost. A new sheet with nothing written on it is never saved.
- **Company settings > Workings**: on/off, sheets per person (200), size of one sheet (256 KB). Off stops new sheets only; existing ones stay with their owners.
- **Exact maths**: decimals are exact (0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3). A result keeps every decimal and shows at most four. No hidden rounding unless `round()` is used.
- **Scratch only**: nothing on a sheet touches the books, and the page says so.

## Privacy
Same model as My pictures: owner-only row-level security, so no role opens someone else's sheets (a director sees nothing, tested). Real delete, no audit lines, no sharing yet (phase 2).

## Also fixed
My pictures had no side padding on phones (it ran to the screen edges). It now has the same padding as other pages.

# B21 — The Guide (business rules, dos and don'ts, how-tos)

**Reading (everyone):** Menu > Guide > Rules and guides. Categories on the side (chips on a phone), search, "To read" marks, a banner with how many are waiting. Open an article, read it, press "I have read and understood this" (recorded per person and per version).
**Writing (permission `guide:manage`, Directors by default; give it to anyone through roles):** Menu > Guide > Write the guide.
- Categories (add, rename, delete when empty) and articles (Draft / Published, kind: Rule, Dos and don'ts, How to, Good to know, "everyone must read" switch).
- Editor made of blocks: Heading, Text (bold/italic buttons), List (bullets, steps, green Do list, red Don't list), Callout (Good to know / Careful / Never / Always), Picture (photo, screenshot or drawing; shrunk to 1600px, stored inside the article), Flowchart (steps, yes/no questions, finish), Line.
- **Live preview** beside the editor (Phone / Computer), drawn by the very same components readers get.
- Publish: tells everyone (optional). Republish: "small fix" (nobody is asked again) or "the rule changed" (version goes up, everyone is told and must read it again).
- **Who has read**: progress bar per article and a list of every active person, unread first.
- Two directors editing the same article: the second save is refused (EDIT_CONFLICT) instead of overwriting.
- **Reminders** (Write the guide page): off / daily / weekly (pick the day) / monthly (1st), at a time in the company's timezone, to people with something unread or to everyone. A background worker (every minute) sends the notification "Time to read the business rules"; once a day at most.
**Ring:** `web/public/sounds/notify.mp3` plays when a notification arrives. Personal switch in My settings > Notifications (with a "Play it" button). I could not download a sound file from the sandbox, so this is a short bell I generated (two soft notes, 9 KB). Replace the file with any mp3 of your choice, same name.

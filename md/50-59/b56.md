# B56 — Cancel a purchase saved by mistake

## What staff get
- On a purchase's page, a red **Cancel purchase** button (only for people with the new permission **Cancel purchases**, given to the Manager and Director roles; rated high risk in the role editor).
- It asks for a reason (at least 5 letters) and a tick: "I have the gold in hand, and this cannot be undone". A list says what will happen.
- On confirm, in one step:
  - every money and gold entry of the purchase is reversed (the books go back exactly as before it; nothing is deleted);
  - the cash goes back into the drawer **of the day open today** (not the original day, which may be closed);
  - the gold leaves stock; a debt the purchase created is written off; a debt it settled with gold is reopened (a negative repayment row); collateral it converted goes back on hold;
  - the purchase stays in the list as **Cancelled**, with its receipt number (never reused) and the reason;
  - reviewers of the branch are notified (except the person who cancelled).
- **Refused when:** any piece of gold has left the branch (GOODS_MOVED), mobile money was already confirmed as sent (MONEY_SENT, reverse it with the provider first), no day is open (NO_OPEN_DAY), already cancelled, or the reason is too short.
- A pending mobile-money part is marked failed. A purchase still waiting in the offline queue cannot be cancelled until it syncs; cancelling needs a connection.

## How to test by hand
1. Make a cash purchase, note the drawer. Open it > Cancel purchase > reason > tick > confirm. Drawer returns; the purchase shows Cancelled; stock lower by its pieces.
2. Try again: refused (already cancelled).
3. Make a purchase, transfer its gold out, try cancelling: refused (gold moved).
4. As a teller without the permission: no button.
5. Purchase paid partly by debt/gold: cancel and check the customer's debt page.

# B54 — Global search

One search for everything a person may see, from any screen.

## What staff get
- **Where:** a "Search…" box in the header on a computer, a round search button in the header on a phone. **Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac) from anywhere, or **/** when not typing. On a computer it opens as a panel over the page; on a phone it takes the whole screen with the keyboard up.
- **What it finds:** customers, purchases, debts and loans, gold held as security, transfers, mobile money, users, branches, guide articles, and pages and actions ("close the day", "momo", "new purchase"). Each person only gets the groups their permissions allow, in the branches they work in.
- **How it matches:**
  - every word typed must be found, in any order, any capitals: `serwaa ama`, `ama 0244`;
  - a phone number in any format: `0244 123 456`, `+233244123456`, `244-123-456`, or just part of it;
  - a receipt number or only its last digits: `0003`;
  - an amount paid: `8605` or `8,605`;
  - a slightly misspelt customer name is still found (`kwezi mensa`), marked "Close match" and listed below exact finds.
- **Results you can recognise without opening them:**
  - customer: phone, number of purchases, last purchase and date, and in red what they owe (only for people who may see debts);
  - purchase: receipt number, date and time, branch, top and down weight, karat, amount, "Cancelled" / "Flagged";
  - debt: who owes whom, reason, since when, outstanding "of" the original amount; held gold: weight and status; transfer: from → to, collector, status; mobile money: reference, purchase, amount, status.
- **Getting around it:** what was typed is highlighted. Chips under the box narrow to one group, with counts. "See all 23" on a group loads more of it. Arrow keys move, **Enter** opens, **Shift+Enter** on a customer starts a new purchase for them (on a phone: the round + button on the row). Esc or Cancel closes.
- **Before typing:** the last things you opened from the search ("Recently opened", kept per person on the device, wiped with "Forget this device"), and shortcuts to the usual places.
- **No connection:** pages and actions still work, and so do this branch's customers and recent purchases held on the device (the same copies the customer picker and the transactions list use). A yellow line says so. A customer found offline leads to a new purchase for them, since their page needs a connection.
- A person who may not open customer pages (a teller without that permission) is taken to a new purchase for the customer instead.

## Decisions I made (say if you want any changed)
- Customers are found company-wide (like the Customers screen), but what they owe is added up only for the caller's branches. Purchases, debts, held gold, transfers and mobile money only from the caller's branches.
- Who may search what: customers = customers:read or transactions:read; purchases = transactions:read; debts and held gold = debts:read; transfers = transfers:read; mobile money = payments:confirm or transactions:read; users = users:manage; branches = branches:manage; guide = everyone.
- Debts, held gold and users open their list page (they have no page of their own); a debt opens the customer's page when there is one.
- Search answers are not kept on the device (only the "recently opened" list is).
- Nothing is searched until two characters are typed, and the server is asked once per pause in typing, not per letter.

## One thing to watch when installing
The migration runs `create extension pg_trgm`. It is part of standard PostgreSQL and normally installs without special rights. If the update stops with "permission denied to create extension", run this once and update again:
`docker compose exec db psql -U postgres -d gold -c "create extension if not exists pg_trgm"` (use your database name).

## How to test by hand
1. Press Ctrl+K. Type a customer's first name: the customer and their purchases appear. Type the surname first, then the first name: same result.
2. Type their phone as `0244 123 456`, then with `+233`: found both ways.
3. Type the last four digits of a receipt number; type an amount you paid today.
4. Misspell a name by one letter: "Close match".
5. Arrow down to a customer, press Shift+Enter: New transaction opens with that customer chosen.
6. Type `close`, `momo`, `drawer`: pages first.
7. Sign in as a teller: no users, branches or debts groups; a customer shows no "owes".
8. Phone: tap the search button; full screen; tap + on a customer.
9. Network off: type a customer's name and `close`; the yellow line shows; results come from the device.

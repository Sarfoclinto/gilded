# B55 — Gold market: the world price chart and our own price history

## What staff get

- **A new page, "Gold market"** (menu: Market), for everyone who can see transactions:
  - **Today:** our price per pound (and since when), the last change, the GoldBod price, our margin.
  - **World gold price, live:** TradingView's chart of gold in US dollars per ounce, with 1 hour / 4 hours / Day / Week. A yellow line under the title says it is for watching only and is never the price we pay.
  - **Our price per pound:** a chart of what this branch has paid over 7 days / 30 days / 3 months / 1 year (gold line), the GoldBod price under it (dashed), and bars for the cedis paid for gold each day. Highest, lowest and number of changes below.
  - **What we bought:** purchases, cedis paid, top weight, and the average price per pound actually paid (weighted by weight).
  - **Hold the world price against ours:** type the world price and the dollar rate; it shows a pound of pure gold in cedis at the world price, and our price as a percentage of it. Nothing is fetched; a rough guide only.
  - **Price changes:** the last 12 changes with GoldBod, margin, price and the step up or down.
- **A "Market" tab in the tools panel** (the round button): our price and its last move, a month of it, the world chart in small, and a link to the page.
- **No connection:** our own price chart and figures still show, as the device last received them (it says how old). The world chart says it needs a connection.
- **Company settings > Gold market:** "Show the live world gold price chart". On by default (your choice); a director can switch it off, and then no outside service is contacted. Our own charts are not affected.
- The search finds the page ("market", "world gold price", "chart").
- The tools panel tabs are a little smaller so six names fit without being cut off.

## Added after your first look: the world chart's size

- **Why it was a thin strip:** TradingView's script sets its own box to "100% high", which threw away the height I had given it. The height is now held by a frame around the chart, so it shows at its proper size (about 520 px on the page, 300 in the tools panel).
- **Chart size** buttons under the chart on the page: **Normal**, **Tall** (about 880 px, never more than the screen) and **Full screen** (covers the whole window; Esc or "Close full screen" leaves it). Normal/Tall is remembered on the device. Changing size does not reload the chart.
- Checked in a browser with a stand-in for TradingView's script that does the same thing to the page: 494 px normal, 766 px tall on a 900 px screen, 802 px full screen, back with Esc, remembered after a reload. Not checked with the real widget (my sandbox cannot reach it).
- Changed: web/src/features/market/WorldGoldChart.tsx, pages/GoldMarketPage.tsx.

## About TradingView (please read)

- The world chart is TradingView's embeddable widget. It loads from their servers and shows their name; that attribution is left exactly as it comes, which their terms require.
- Their terms also say commercial use needs a separate agreement and do not say clearly whether a paid app behind a login counts. You chose to include it with a switch. Please email TradingView to confirm; if they say no, switch it off in Company settings and nothing else changes.
- Our own charts use TradingView's open-source "Lightweight Charts" (Apache-2.0). Its licence asks for a link to TradingView; the small logo on our chart is that link. Do not hide it.
- Sources: https://www.tradingview.com/policies/ , https://www.tradingview.com/widget-docs/tutorials/build-page/widget-integration/ , https://www.tradingview.com/free-charting-libraries/

## How to test by hand

1. Menu > Gold market. The four tiles match Prices for your branch.
2. The world chart shows gold moving; switch 1 hour / Day / Week. Check the TradingView name shows.
3. Our price chart: change the period; the last value equals today's price. Change a margin on Prices, come back: a new step and a new row under Price changes.
4. Make a purchase: "What we bought" and today's bar go up.
5. Type a world price and a dollar rate in the comparison box.
6. Round tools button > Market.
7. Company settings > Gold market: switch the world chart off; it disappears from the page and the panel; our charts stay.
8. Network off: the page still shows our chart with the "as this device last received" line; the world chart says it needs a connection.
9. Desktop app: the same checks (the world chart loads from the internet there too).

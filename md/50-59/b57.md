# B57 — Transfer totals and the scaled weight

## What staff get

- **Choosing gold to send:** under the list, a clear panel: **Total top**, **Total down** and **Worth** of what is ticked (by the books).
- **Hand to the collector (gold):** the form shows the books' top and down, and asks the sender to put **all the pieces on the scale together** and type the **scaled top** and **scaled down**. It is required for gold. As soon as both are typed, the form shows the books against the scale, the difference (+/−), and a colour:
  - **Top:** exact = green · up to 0.10 g = **yellow** · up to 0.50 g = **orange** · over 0.50 g = **red**.
  - **Down** (your "come up with something"): the allowed gap grows with the number of pieces and their weight: `0.02 + 0.01 per piece + 0.001 per gram of top`, never more than 0.50 g. Up to half of it = green · up to all of it = yellow · beyond it = orange · over 0.50 g = red.
    Examples: 1 piece of 7.77 g allows 0.04 g; 2 pieces of 15.54 g allow 0.06 g; 60 pieces / 400 g allow the full 0.50 g.
  - The worse of top and down gives the overall colour.
- **Nothing is refused:** the transfer is always released and the scale is recorded beside the books (the books' figures are never changed).
- **Red also notifies reviewers** of the sending branch (people with "review discrepancies"). Orange and yellow are only shown.
- **On the transfer page:** the "Sent" card shows the scale and its differences in the grade colour, and when it is not green a panel at the top shows the books against the scale again. Everyone who opens the transfer sees it, including the receiving branch.
- The audit trail records every reading (`transfer.scaled`).

## Decisions to check

- The limits are fixed in the database function `gold.scale_check` (0058), not settings. Tell me if you want them editable per company.
- Cash transfers have no scale. Older desktop/web builds that release without a reading still work (no scale block).
- Only red notifies. Say so if orange should too.

## How to test by hand

1. New transfer > tick gold: Total top / down / Worth update.
2. Release: type the exact books figures (green), +0.05 top (yellow), +0.3 (orange), +0.9 (red). Release the red one; a reviewer sees a notification; the transfer page shows the red panel.
3. With many pieces, check a 0.2 g down gap is tolerated, and with one piece it is orange.

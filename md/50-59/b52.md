# B52 — Offline day open/close, step 2 of 3: the screens

Needs B51 (server, migration 0053) first. Design: docs/OFFLINE-DAYS-DESIGN.md.
After this, **one computer can open and close days with no internet** and keep working across days. Branches with several computers sharing a drawer through a hub come in B53 (see "Limits").

## What changes for staff
- **Open the day with no connection.** The page says "No connection to the server", shows *this computer's* books (the server's last figures plus everything made here since), and the day opens on this computer at once. It is sent when there is a connection.
- **Close the day with no connection.** The seven figures are worked out on the computer: cash from the drawer; gold top, down and value from the purchases made; money owed and debts settled with gold from those purchases; debts and held gold as last heard from the server. Reasons are asked against those figures, the confirmation is ticked as usual, and the closing is saved here and sent in order.
- **Several days in a row.** Close Monday, open Tuesday, buy, pay, cash in/out, close Tuesday, open Wednesday: all with no connection. Everything is sent in the order it was made.
- **Entries still waiting.** If this computer still holds entries the server has not received, opening/closing is saved here and sent after them (before, the page asked you to wait). The old "wait for them to sync before closing" warning is gone.
- **Connection drops while sending:** the opening or closing is saved on the computer instead; nothing is lost and it cannot be recorded twice.
- The drawer page shows "On this computer · not sent yet" while a day's opening or closing has not reached the server.
- When they arrive, the server treats them as B51 describes: never refused, differences go to the manager.
- With a connection and nothing waiting, opening and closing work exactly as before (sent straight away, strict rule).

## Guards
- A computer that has never loaded the drawer cannot open a day offline ("connect once").
- A computer whose date is before the last day it knows is stopped and told to correct its clock.
- A computer whose stored copy is from before this update has no book figures yet: closing offline says "connect once" until it has been online once after the update.
- If gold held as security was bought offline, the closing page warns that the held-gold figure may be off (the computer cannot know the weight released).

## Limits (to know)
- **Hub branches (several computers, one drawer):** an opening or closing made offline on one computer is not yet shown on the others. Until B53, open and close offline from one computer and keep trading on that one, or wait for B53 before relying on it in those branches.
- "Day summary · this day only" on the closing page still needs a connection.
- Mobile money parts made offline are not in the books until confirmed, as online.

## How to test by hand (one computer; laptop with the local server is fine)
1. Online: sign in, open the day, make a purchase. Open the Close page once (this stores the confirmation wording), go back without closing.
2. Turn the network off (or stop the server). Make a purchase and a cash-in.
3. Close the day: the page says "No connection", shows the books; cash should equal the drawer, gold value should include both purchases. Count, tick, close. Drawer page: "This day is closed · On this computer · not sent yet".
4. Change the computer's date to tomorrow (or wait): Open the day: "This computer's books say the drawer holds ..." equals what you counted. Open with a different count on purpose and write a reason. Make a purchase.
5. Turn the network on. The header's waiting count should fall to 0. Check Day history: yesterday closed (flag "Closed while offline"), today open ("Opened while offline"). Purchases, cash-in in the right days.
6. Repeat 2–3 but, before reconnecting, make a purchase for the same branch from a browser on another device (online). On reconnect the closing still goes in; the manager gets "An offline closing count ... did not match the books", the day shows "A difference was found when the offline count arrived" and waits for sign-off.
7. Set the computer's date to last week and try to open: it is stopped with the clock message.

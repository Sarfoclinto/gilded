# B58 — Branch performance over time

## What staff get
- **Dashboard (managers and directors): "Branch performance".** One coloured line per branch on a shared time axis.
  - Switch what the lines show: **Paid out** (cedis paid for gold), **Weight** (pounds bought) or **Purchases** (how many).
  - Pick the period: **7, 14, 30 days** (a point per day), **3 months** (a point per week, Mondays) or **12 months** (a point per month).
  - The headline: the total for the branches shown, how it compares with the same length of time before (green up / red down), the best day/week/month, and the average per day/week/month.
  - **Branch chips** under the headline carry each branch's total and its change. Click a chip to hide that branch; double-click to see it alone. Hiding never recolours the others: each branch keeps its colour everywhere (branches are coloured in code order).
  - **Hover or tap** anywhere: a hairline snaps to the nearest date and a read-out lists every branch at that date, biggest first, with the total for all shown. Arrow keys do the same from the keyboard.
  - Weekends are shaded on the daily charts; Sundays show as the zero they are.
  - Up to four visible lines are named at their right-hand end (branch code), so colour is never the only clue.
  - **"Show the numbers"** opens the same data as a table (newest first, with totals).
  - The metric and period you choose are remembered on the device.
- **Tellers:** the same panel on their home, titled "How this branch is doing", with only their own branch (no chips).
- **Dates now show the weekday** where people read them: the chart's axis ("Mon 3"), its read-out ("Monday 3 Nov 2026"), and the shared formats used across the app: `when` ("Mon 02 Nov, 14:05" — transaction lists, receipts' saved time, drawer activity, notifications, payments, debts, transfers), `day` ("Fri 2 Jan 2026") and the dashboard's bar chart dates ("Tue 20 Oct"). If any screen's column is now too narrow for the longer text, tell me which.
- Cancelled purchases never count (B56 cancellations drop out of the chart at once).

## Decisions to check
- "Weight" is in **pounds**, like the rest of the dashboard and the price per pound. The server also sends the top weight in grams if you would rather see grams; say so.
- Each purchase counts on its **branch's own calendar day**; the 12-month view's first and last months are part months (the footer says so, and the read-out says "(so far)" for the current one).
- The colours are the dataviz palette checked for colour-blind readers (blue, orange, aqua, yellow, violet, pink, green, red). A ninth branch repeats the colours with a dashed line.

## How to test by hand
1. Log in as a director: the new panel sits under "Needs your attention". Switch Paid out / Weight / Purchases and the periods; the headline and chips follow.
2. Hover the chart; tap on a phone; use ← → on the keyboard.
3. Click a chip to hide a branch (the others keep their colours); double-click to see one alone; click again to bring all back.
4. "Show the numbers": the table matches the read-out.
5. Cancel a purchase (B56): today's point drops.
6. Log in as a teller: "How this branch is doing" shows one line, no chips.
7. Reload: the metric and period are remembered.

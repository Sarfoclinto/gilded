# B15c — recall a transfer that never arrived

**Why:** a released transfer could only end by being received. If the carrier never arrived, the gold or cash stayed "in transit" and the sending branch stayed short forever.

**What it does:** the sending branch can **Recall** a released, not-yet-received transfer, with a reason. What left comes back into its books through new ledger entries (nothing edited), items go back on the shelf and can be sent again, the transfer closes as cancelled ("Recalled: reason"), and the receiving branch is notified not to accept it. A recall and a receipt cannot both win (the transfer row is locked). Needs an open day at the sending branch.

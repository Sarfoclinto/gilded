# B74 - Group Sheets, phase 1

Private running records for a chosen group: kitchen fees, mercury sales, a welfare pot. The design and the seven decisions are in `docs/group-sheets-design.md`.

## What changed
- **Groups.** People with `groups:create` (directors and managers by default; a company can give it to anyone) create a group and become its first keeper. Members join in two ways:
  - **by name**, as keeper, writer or reader;
  - **"everyone at a branch"**, as writers or readers. This follows branch assignments only (never `branches:all`): people posted there join, people who leave drop out. A keeper can leave one person out of a branch and let them back in.
  - Keepers are always chosen by name.
- **No company role opens a group.** Directors and admins see nothing (no link, no names, no figures) unless they are added. This is enforced by row-level security in the database, tested directly.
  - The single exception: a group whose last keeper's account is switched off. People with `users:manage` get a notification, see that group's name and remaining members only, and name a new keeper from them. The group's log shows who did it.
  - The app warns while a group has only one keeper.
- **Sheets.** Each sheet has a name, an optional branch, and an optional opening balance with its date. Keepers can rename, close (readable, no new entries) and reopen a sheet. A sheet can be deleted only while it has no entries.
- **Entries.**
  - **+ Add** or **− Take out**, with an amount, an optional note (140 characters), the date the money moved, and a **Day total** switch.
  - Taking out more than the balance is allowed, with a red warning.
  - Every line shows who wrote it and when, and a late entry also shows when it was written.
  - **Nothing is edited or deleted.** The app login cannot even update or delete these rows. A mistake is **taken back**: a linked opposite entry on the same date, both shown struck through with who did it. Writers take back their own; keepers anyone's.
  - A taking back and the line it cancels are left out of the day's in and out (they net to zero). The balance is the same either way.
- **Who sees what.** By default, writers see and write every sheet and readers see every sheet. On the **Who sees what** tab (a table of people against sheets), a keeper can narrow any sheet for one person or for everyone: *see and write*, *see only* or *not see*. Narrowing never makes a reader a writer. A hidden sheet simply does not exist for that person.
- **Offline.**
  - **Entries:** written with no connection, an entry is kept on the device for that person and shows "Waiting to send" with the balance marked "on this device". It is sent in order when the connection returns, and kept once even if sent twice.
  - **Refused entries:** an entry the server refuses is shown with the reason and a "Remove from this device" button.
  - **Entries after removal:** an entry written before its writer lost access is accepted and marked "Sent after the writer lost access" (decision 3), and the group's log says so.
  - **Group data on shared computers:** none of it is put in the cache that survives sign-out. Each person's last-seen copy is kept under their own id, and it is dropped when the server says the sheet is gone.
- **Activity tab** for every member: the group's log in sentences (who joined or left, roles, branch rules, sheets, access changes, takings back, late entries).
- **Menu.** "Group Sheets" sits under Operations, next to Workings, and is shown only to people who may create groups, are in a group, or must name a keeper. Search finds it under "kitchen fees", "mercury", "savings" and similar words.
- **Not touched:** the cash drawer, day closing, purchases, debts and company reports (decision 4). Backups include the new tables automatically.

## Tested
- **Database:** the full suite passes. The new test 065 has 14 parts:
  - who may create a group;
  - the director (with `branches:all` and `users:manage`) sees nothing, even reading the tables directly;
  - branch rules, leaving one person out;
  - opening balance, entries, sending twice, bad amounts and future dates;
  - entries cannot be edited or deleted;
  - readers cannot add;
  - per-sheet hiding;
  - taking back (own only for writers, once only);
  - entries after removal (accepted when written before, refused after);
  - the last keeper cannot leave or step down;
  - the no-keeper case (notification, names only, naming a keeper, then gone again);
  - closing and deleting;
  - broken ids.
- **Web:**
  - `npm run typecheck` is clean;
  - all 69 test files pass (414 tests), including the menu and role catalogue tests and 8 new Group Sheets tests (amounts, waiting entries, takings back, activity sentences).
- **End to end in a browser, against the real database functions** (a small local stand-in for the API that calls `gold.group_sheets`, in place of the C#):
  - the main page, a sheet, the take-out form, People, Who sees what and Activity were checked on desktop and phone, with no sideways scrolling on the phone;
  - an entry added online moved the balance;
  - an entry written offline showed "Waiting to send" and reached the server when the connection came back;
  - a director saw no group, and a direct sheet link said it does not exist.

## Not tested, and how to test
- **The C# was not compiled here** (no .NET in my sandbox). Run `cd backend`, then `dotnet build`, then `dotnet test --filter "GroupSheetsServiceTests|MigrationTests"`. The endpoints are thin: every route calls the same database function the browser test used.
- **On your laptop with the real API:**
  1. Sign in as a manager, open **Group Sheets**, create "Anyanso staff", add everyone at a branch as writers and one reader by name, start "Kitchen fees" with an opening balance.
  2. As a writer on another browser, add +50 and −20.
  3. As the director, check that the menu has no Group Sheets link (unless you give the director's role `groups:create`) and that nothing shows.
  4. Turn Wi-Fi off, add an entry, turn it on, and watch it send.
  5. Switch off the only keeper's account in Users, and check that the director gets "A group has no keeper".
- **Live updates:** other people's entries appear within 30 seconds while the page is open (and at once on reopening), not instantly. Instant updates would be a small addition later if wanted.

# B72 - Company website at gildedgold.org, hosting guide, off-site backup guide

## What changed
- **Company website** (`website/`): one page for customers. It covers how a sale works (weighed in air and in water, priced at the day's rate, paid on the spot) and has these parts:
  - An example reading.
  - Services: buying and refining of gold.
  - Branches, About us, Contact, and a Staff sign-in link to the system.
  - Colours and logo from the shop banner, with the honeycomb mesh and gold lines in the top corner as on the banner.
- **All facts are in one file, `website/content.js`.** A value left `''` is hidden, so the page never shows a blank or a TODO. Already filled from the banner: both phone numbers, the e-mail (to check) and the Anyanso Branch.
- **Hosting:** the same Caddy container serves the site on `WEBSITE_ADDRESS` (gildedgold.org), and `WEBSITE_WWW_ADDRESS` (www) sends a permanent redirect to it. Certificates are automatic. `content.js` and the page are never cached, so changes show at once. Unknown paths show the page. The site files are mounted read-only, so a `git pull` is enough to update it, with no rebuild.
- **Guide 12** `docs/deployment/12-company-website.md`: fill the facts, Namecheap DNS, `.env`, deploy, check, change later, Google Business Profile, free e-mail forwarding, and troubleshooting.
- **Guide 13** `docs/deployment/13-offsite-backups-b2.md`: covers these steps:
  - The Backblaze account, a private bucket, the lifecycle setting "keep only the last version", and a key limited to the bucket.
  - The passphrase and where to keep it.
  - The `.env` lines, starting the service, and removing the old cron line.
  - Proving it works, and practising a restore on the laptop.
  - The day the server is gone.
  - Other S3 providers, and troubleshooting.
- Cross-references in guides 00 and 10 and in `deploy/BACKUPS.md`. Improvement plan item added.

## Tested
- The Caddyfile was validated with Caddy 2.6 in both cases, with and without the WEBSITE_ settings.
- Served locally:
  - the site and the app both work;
  - `content.js` comes back with `no-cache`;
  - an unknown path gives 404 with the page;
  - www gives a 301 and keeps the path.
- Page checked in Chromium at desktop and phone widths, with the real facts and with sample facts: no horizontal scroll, the phone menu opens and closes, no script errors.

## Not tested, and how to test
- **Real DNS and certificates:** follow guide 12 Parts B to D. Look for "certificate obtained successfully" in `docker compose logs web`.
- **Fonts:** the sandbox blocks Google Fonts, so my screenshots show a fallback serif. Check that the headings show in Playfair Display on a real browser.
- **Backblaze end to end:** the B71 scripts were tested locally, but not against a real bucket. Follow guide 13 Part D (`backup-now`, `offsite list`, `restore-check`), then Part E once on the laptop.
- `tel:` and WhatsApp links on a real phone (guide 12 Part D step 4).

# B70: screen lock with a PIN (v1.19.1)

Your choices: lock after 5 minutes with no activity (a company setting), unlock with a 4 to 6 digit PIN or the password, five wrong PINs sign the person out. Needs the database migration (0070) and the web deploy (the desktop app picks it up with the web). No C# change. Based on `feat-panel-debts-hub` (B69).

## What people get
- **Automatic lock.** After the company's idle time (5 minutes unless changed), the screen locks. Any tap, key, scroll or wheel counts as activity, in any open tab of the app, so working in one tab never locks another. A computer that slept is checked the moment it wakes.
- **Lock now** from the account menu (Lock screen) or My settings.
- **The lock screen** is navy, with the Gilded mark, the person's photo and name, why it locked, and a large PIN pad that also takes the keyboard.
  - Wrong PINs shake the dots and say how many tries are left. The fifth wrong PIN signs the person out (unsent entries stay on the device, as with any sign-out).
  - "Use my password instead" (the key on the pad) unlocks with the password; this works with no connection, using the device's stored sign-in check.
  - "Not Ama? Sign out" lets someone else sign in.
- **Nothing is lost.** The lock covers the app without closing anything: a half-filled purchase is exactly as it was after unlocking (tested).
- **It cannot be skipped.** A reload, closing and reopening the tab, or another tab all stay locked until unlocked. Nothing behind the lock can be reached with the keyboard or a screen reader.
- **Setting a PIN.** In My settings > Screen lock (needs the password), or right after the first password unlock ("Choose a PIN for this device", with Not now). Easy PINs (1111, 1234, 9876) are refused. Change or Remove it there too.
- **Company settings > Screen lock:** the idle time, 0 to 60 minutes; 0 means never by itself (people can still lock by hand).

## How the PIN is kept (a design choice to know)
The PIN is kept **on each device**, as a salted hash (never the PIN itself), not on the server. That way it works with no connection, needs no server change, and a PIN seen at one counter does not unlock another. A person sets it once per device they use. It is a guard against someone using a session left open, not a replacement for the password: the account is still protected by the password, and five wrong PINs end the session. If you would rather have one PIN that follows the person to every device, it can be added on the server later.

## Tested
- Database: 63 files pass (default 5, change, 0 allowed, 61 and 2.5 refused).
- Web: tsc clean, vitest 399 pass (new: weak PINs refused, only a hash is stored, another person's PIN does not unlock, lock timing, the lock survives a reload, the try counter).
- In a browser, all against a stand-in server:
  - Lock from the menu with a half-filled purchase.
  - A wrong password, then the right one.
  - "1234" refused, then a PIN chosen and confirmed; the purchase was still filled in after unlocking.
  - Lock again: a wrong PIN said "4 tries left".
  - A reload stayed locked; the PIN unlocked.
  - The idle lock fired after 6 minutes of no activity.
  - Phone and desktop screenshots: b70-lock-pin.png, b70-lock-phone.png, b70-lock-newpin.png, b70-mysettings.png.
  - A bug found and fixed on the way: one wrong PIN was counted twice.
- **Not tested:**
  - A real phone or tablet.
  - The installed desktop app.
  - Two tabs at once by hand (the shared storage is tested in code).
  - A password unlock on a device that has never signed in with the password (it then checks with the server, and needs a connection).

## How to test
1. Company settings > Screen lock: set 1 minute. Leave the app alone for a minute: it locks.
2. Unlock with your password; choose a PIN when asked. Lock again from the account menu; unlock with the PIN.
3. Start a purchase, lock, unlock: the purchase is still there.
4. Lock, then reload the page: still locked.
5. Type a wrong PIN five times: you are signed out.

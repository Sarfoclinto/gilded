# B75 - Group Sheets, phase 2

Savings per person, mercury with a quantity, receipt photos, print and CSV, alerts on money taken out, and a Group tab in the Tools panel. The design is in `docs/GROUP-SHEET-DESIGN.MD` (section 10 now records the choices made).

## What changed
- **Three kinds of sheet.** When a keeper starts a sheet, they choose what it keeps:
  - **Money**: as before.
  - **Savings per person**: one balance for each person, and the total.
    - Keepers add people: someone who uses the app, or anyone by name (for example "Auntie Akos"). Keepers can rename them.
    - A savings sheet starts at zero; each person's existing balance is brought over as their first entry.
    - Every entry says whose savings it is.
    - Tapping a person shows **their statement**: their entries only, their balance brought forward, and their closing balance each day. It prints and downloads like any sheet.
  - **Money and quantity** (mercury): the sheet has a unit (kg, g…) and an optional quantity in hand.
    - An entry can move money, quantity or both. For example: "Sold 0.5 kg: +1,800 cedis, −0.5 kg", "From Kumasi: +1.25 kg", "Paid for it: −15,000".
    - The sheet shows **what is in hand** next to the cash, and each day shows kg in and out, and kg at its close.
    - Taking an entry back reverses its quantity too.
- **Receipt photos.**
  - **Adding:** a photo can be added on the Add / Take out form (camera or library, up to 5), or later with "Add receipt photo" on your own entries (keepers on any).
  - **Offline:** photos go through the app's existing photo queue. They are kept on the phone with no connection, sent when it returns, and wait for their entry to reach the server first.
  - **Privacy:** only people who can see the sheet can open them. They have their own table and their own folder on the server (`tenant/g/entry/…`), and are never in the Photos library or the audit trail. Hiding a sheet from someone hides its photos too.
  - **Removing:** the person who added a photo, or a keeper, can take it off. The group's log says so.
- **Alerts.** On any sheet, the bell sets "Tell me when money is taken out", for any amount or from an amount up.
  - The notification reads like "Ama took GHS 500.00 out of Mercury", with the note, and opens the sheet.
  - It goes only to people who still see the sheet, and never to the person who wrote the entry. Nobody else sees who asked.
- **Print and download.** The printer button prints the sheet on white, with the period and who printed it, and without buttons. The download button saves the period on screen as a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets:
  - one row per entry, with each day's closing balance;
  - kg in and out on quantity sheets;
  - the person's name on savings sheets.

  A **From** date chooses where the period starts.
- **Tools panel → Group.** Every open sheet you can write on, with its balance (and kg in hand) and + Add / − Take out, beside whatever page is open. It uses the same form and the same offline queue as the sheet's page. The tab only shows for people who are in a group or may create one.
- **Smaller things:**
  - The camera on each entry line is now a small "Add receipt photo" link, shown only on your own entries (and to keepers).
  - The main page's cards show kg in hand (mercury) and how many people save (savings).

## Tested
- **Database:** the full suite passes. The new test 066 covers:
  - savings: people, unique names, keepers only, an entry must name its person, balances per person, a statement with its balance brought forward;
  - quantity: unit required, opening stock, quantity-only entries, stock, taking back reverses the quantity;
  - photos: sent twice and kept once; the director can neither open nor add one; a writer cannot remove someone else's; a hidden sheet hides its photo; a keeper removes one and the log says so;
  - alerts: from an amount up, any amount, never to the writer, the wording, and none once the sheet is hidden from that person.
- **Web:** `npm run typecheck` is clean, and all 70 test files pass (419 tests). New tests cover quantity, statements and CSV quoting.
- **End to end in a browser, against the real database functions** (a stand-in for the C# that calls `gold.group_sheets`, photos included):
  - a mercury entry with only a quantity moved "in hand" from 2.25 kg to 2.75 kg;
  - a savings statement showed Auntie Akos at GHS 1,000;
  - the CSV downloaded with the right columns;
  - a receipt photo went through the offline queue, was stored with its preview, and showed on the entry;
  - the director got "not found" for the photo;
  - the Tools panel Group tab worked;
  - phone widths had no sideways scrolling.

## Not tested, and how to test
- **The C# was not compiled here** (no .NET in my sandbox). Run:
  ```
  cd backend
  dotnet build
  dotnet test --filter "GroupSheetsServiceTests|MigrationTests"
  ```
- **On a real phone:**
  1. Take a receipt photo with the camera from the Take out form.
  2. Turn Wi-Fi off, add an entry with a photo, turn it back on, and watch both arrive.
  3. Check the photo opens for another member and not for a director.
- **The alert notification** in the app's bell, with two people signed in.
- **Printing** from your real browser (the print layout was only checked through the code, not on paper).

# B73 - GoldBod's world price, always shown, with an up or down arrow

## What changed
- **GoldBod's world price is always on show.** This is the figure on GoldBod's page, the LBMA price in US dollars per ounce that its price is built on (e.g. `USD 3,759.35`). It shows with the time GoldBod published it and its dollar rate (`USD 1 = GHS 11.55`). It appears:
  - on the Gold market page, above the world chart;
  - in the side panel's Market tab, above the small chart.
- **Held against the price now.** Under it: "Now USD 3,781.20 +21.85 (0.58%)". A round badge shows a **green arrow up and to the right** when the world price has risen since GoldBod published, and a **red arrow down and to the right** when it has fallen. The chart below still shows the trend.
- **Where "now" comes from.** The TradingView chart cannot hand its price to our page: it runs in its own sealed frame and has no way to read it. So the API asks **xaus.com** (free, no key, allowed for dashboards like ours) for the spot price.
  - It asks **at most once a minute**, however many people have the page open. Pages refresh it every minute.
  - If xaus.com is down, the last good price is shown for up to 30 minutes, marked "last fetched …". After that the tile says the price could not be fetched, and there is no arrow.
  - Offline there is no "now" and no arrow, because an old price would mislead. GoldBod's figure still shows, from the history kept on the device.
  - If a director has switched the outside chart off (Company settings), nothing outside is asked. GoldBod's figure still shows, without the comparison.
- The GoldBod figure was already stored with every price read from GoldBod. Migration 0071 only returns the newest one with the price history (`goldbod`). Prices typed in by hand carry no dollar figure, so they never hide it.

## Not tested, and how to test
- **The C# was not compiled here** (no .NET in my sandbox). On your laptop:
  ```
  cd backend
  dotnet build
  dotnet test --filter WorldPriceTests
  ```
- **The real xaus.com answer.** The parser follows their published example (`spot_usd_oz`, `updated_at`). After deploying, sign in and open `https://app.gildedgold.org/api/v1/market/world-price`. Expect `"available":true` and a price close to the TradingView chart. The two may differ by a few dollars: different sources, both indicative.
- In the app, open Gold market. The tile shows GoldBod's last figure, and after about a second "Now USD …" with the arrow. Turn Wi-Fi off: the arrow goes and the GoldBod figure stays.

# B46 - "Closing" tab: what my closing account would be if I closed now

## What it does

The Tools panel (round button) has a new **Closing** tab, for anyone who can see the cash (the same permission as the Cash tab: `days:read`), for the branch they are working in. It refreshes when opened and every minute, and has a Refresh button.

- **Cash:** the total, then **Bundles** (whole 100s of notes, by note) and **Loose** (the rest). If the note counts in the books are not sound (for example a branch with the note rule off has gone negative on a note) it shows **the amount only**, with a short line saying so.
- **Debtors:** each person who owes the branch, with the amount; "Other (not listed by name)" if the books hold debt that has no named debtor.
- **Goods in stock:** the amount, with how many transactions it is made of and the total top/down weights. Tap it to open the list: each purchase with customer, date and time, reference, top, down and value, newest first. Anything the books hold beyond those purchases appears as "Brought forward / other". "Stock" means purchases since goods were last sent out of the branch; cancelled purchases are left out.
- **Owed to customers** (only when there is something) and the **Closing balance** = cash + debtors + goods − owed to customers (the same formula as the daily account).
- Read-only: it never closes or changes anything. If no day is open it says so and shows where the books stand after the last close.

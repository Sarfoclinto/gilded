## Rules
- director, manager and teller are fixed: copy one to start your own. Custom roles can be renamed, changed and deleted.
- A custom role cannot be deleted while anyone (even switched off) holds it.
- Changing what a role allows signs its holders out, so it applies at once.
- Nobody can change their own role; the company must always keep one active person who can manage users (the change is refused otherwise).
- Every role change is in the audit trail with before and after.

## Assessment (computed on the screen, not stored)
Sensitive access (high/care), duties that usually belong to different people (records purchases + signs off; sets prices + approves them; counts the drawer + decides differences), missing basics with an "Add it" button, and people in the role with personal exceptions. The lists of what counts as sensitive or clashing are in `features/roles/catalog.ts`: please review them and tell me what to change.

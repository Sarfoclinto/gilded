# B45 - "Customer" tab in the utility panel: the customer's recent purchases

## What it does
- The round button (bottom right) now opens the **Tools** panel (renamed from "Calculators"). New tab **Customer**.
- On the New transaction page, whatever is in the customer box (a picked customer, or a name / phone being typed) is shared with the panel. **Tapping the button then opens straight on the Customer tab.** With no customer entered it opens on the tab you last used, as before.
- The tab lists the customer's latest 8 purchases, newest first. Each card: date and time, the reference, then every piece with **Top**, **Down** and the **carat** (amber, bold), and the amount last, in light text (tap it to open the purchase). Cancelled purchases are faded and say so.
- Lookup: a picked customer is found by id. If only a name or phone was typed: a phone of 6+ digits is searched first, otherwise the name.
- Nothing found: `Customer "x" does not exist.` (nobody by that name/phone) or `No transactions yet for "x".` (the customer exists but has no purchases).
- Read-only: the panel never changes the customer on the page. The shared customer is cleared when you leave the New transaction page.
- Needs a connection (it asks the server); offline it says so instead of showing stale data.

## Decisions to know about
- Purchases from all branches the person is allowed to see are listed (a customer may sell at several branches). The server decides what they may see; no backend change was needed.
- A typed name is matched by the server's normal search, so "Kofi" can also list a "Kofi Mensah"'s purchases. Pick the customer from the list to be exact.

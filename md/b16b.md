# B16b — "what carat to expect" hint (step 2 of the town idea)

**What it does:** on the New transaction page, once a town is chosen (and/or an existing customer picked), a small "Expect" note appears: *"Obuasi is usually 22 high (mostly 22 mid to 23 low) · 47 pieces"*, and the same for the customer ("Kwesi usually brings…"). It is a guide only: it never changes the price or the result. With fewer than 5 pieces it says only how many have been bought, so a guess from two purchases is never shown as a rule. Cancelled purchases are left out. It needs a connection and stays silent offline.

**How it is worked out:** plain statistics over this company's own purchases (median and the 10th–90th percentile range of the karat), said the way tellers say it: 22.1 = "22 low", 22.5 = "22 mid", 22.8 = "22 high". No model. If the numbers ever prove too rough we can think about more.

**Not in this batch:** the news feed, the "unusual for this town" flag on the measured result, and rename/merge for towns.

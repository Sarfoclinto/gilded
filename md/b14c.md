# B14c: the customer receipt

Front end only. No migration, no backend change.

## What it does
- Each purchase has a designed receipt: business header, receipt number, date, customer, served-by, every item (top, down, density, pounds, carat, net weight, price per pound and amount), value of gold, the rounding line, a boxed "Amount paid to you", how it was paid (cash / mobile money / owed) and the notes given, a QR code and a footer. Cancelled purchases are stamped CANCELLED. A purchase still waiting to be sent prints too, marked "Recorded on this device".
- Paper: 80 mm roll, 58 mm roll or A4. Pick it on the page; it is remembered on this device.
- Works with or without a printer: the same page is the on-screen receipt, and the Print button uses whatever printer the device has (including "Save as PDF").
- Own address with no menu or header: `/transactions/:id/receipt`. Add `?print=1` to print as soon as it loads, `?bare=1` to hide the toolbar, `?paper=80|58|a4` to force a size. A future Electron shell can open this address in a hidden window and call `webContents.print` with the exact roll size.
- The QR code holds `gilded:tx:<id>:<reference>` (no amounts). It is there for the paying desk in the two-step payout.
- The purchase page's Print button is now "Receipt" and opens this page.

## Known limits
- Browsers cannot size a page to its content, so on a roll the printer's own roll size applies. Electron can set it exactly.
- Business name, address, phone and footer are kept on this device for now (defaults in `receipt/settings.ts`). They move to the admin company settings page when that is built.
- The QR code is not scanned by anything yet.

## What it records

Purchases saved and voided; days opened, closed, signed off or rejected; drawer top-ups and withdrawals; transfers drafted, released, received (with or without a difference) and cancelled; difference-case actions; loans, repayments, write-offs, held gold; mobile payments confirmed or failed; margins set and approved and price-feed events; user, customer and branch changes (with before and after).

## Not recorded (honest limits)

- Sign-ins and failed sign-ins, and who merely looked at something: nothing stores them yet.
- Edits are listed only where the original record keeps who and when. A record changed in place, other than the user/customer/branch changes above, shows its latest state, not each step.
- Company-wide events (user, customer, branch changes) have no branch, so someone limited to certain branches does not see them.

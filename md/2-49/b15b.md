# B15b: settings pages (company and personal)

Front end only. Needs B15a (migration 0038 and the settings API) applied first. No migration here.

## Two pages, for everyone including admins
- **My settings** (`/settings/me`, from the account menu, for every signed-in person): your name, role and branches, change password; receipts at this desk (paper, open the receipt after saving, weights, QR code); slim menu. Kept on this device; they only adjust what the company decided.
- **Company settings** (`/settings/company`, needs `settings:manage`, directors by default; in the menu under Setup and in the account menu): one **tab per group**, with a dot on any tab holding unsaved changes and a sticky Save / Discard bar.
  - Paying customers: two-step payout (asks for confirmation when switched on), the recorder may also pay, payer re-checks the weights.
  - Receipts: company uses a printer, default paper, with a live sample receipt beside the fields.
  - Business details: name, line under the name, address, phone, footer, with the same live sample.
  - Cash and allowance: office allowance per person (now used by the cash drawer instead of the fixed 100).
- The admin therefore has both pages, like anyone else.

## Adding a setting later
Add the key to `gold.setting_defaults()` and its validation in `gold.set_settings` (a small migration), add it to the schema in `features/settings/api.ts`, and add one entry to `SECTIONS` in `features/settings/model.ts`. A new section there becomes a new tab by itself. A test checks every server setting appears exactly once.

## Behaviour changes you will notice
- Receipts take the business name, address, phone and footer from the company settings (a plain "Gold Buyers" until written).
- With **uses a receipt printer** on, saving a purchase opens its receipt ready to print (`?print=1`). Each person can override this for their desk.
- The cash drawer's allowance uses the company's amount per person.

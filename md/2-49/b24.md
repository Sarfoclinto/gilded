# B24 — Continue an unfinished purchase

A purchase that was started and not saved is kept on the device (per person and branch) a moment after each change: customer, phone, representative, town, every item (weights and price) and the payment split. Leaving the page (to check a customer, answer the phone) loses nothing.
- Coming back to New transaction restores it, with a blue line "Continuing the purchase you started at 14:53" and a **Start over** button.
- The header button says **Continue purchase** (desktop) and the raised phone button says **Continue** while one is waiting.
- Cleared on Save, on Start over, and automatically after 12 hours (another working day). An untouched form (only today's price filled in) is not kept.
- The same transaction id is kept, so Save twice still creates one purchase. Held-gold purchases ("Refine and buy") are never restored into.
- Kept in this browser only (localStorage key `gold.purchaseDraft.v1`); nothing is sent to the server or recorded in the books until Save.
- Not kept: the notes chosen in the payout panel (it recalculates), and the Classic/Focus choice is separate (already personal).

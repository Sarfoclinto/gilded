# B15a: two-step payout (one person records, another pays) and company settings

## What it does

Off by default, so nothing changes until you switch it on. When on:

1. **Record:** the teller weighs, saves the purchase and (optionally) prints the receipt. The gold goes into stock. The cash is booked as _owed to the customer_ and does **not** leave the drawer. The people who can pay are notified.
2. **Pay:** at the paying desk, open **Transactions > To pay**. Newest purchases are at the top, with search by receipt number, name or phone. Tap Pay, optionally check the weights (top, down, price) the way you do in your sheet, choose the notes (they start as the recorder's suggestion), hand over the cash. The cash leaves the drawer by note at that moment.

- **The amount can never change at the paying step.** The notes must add up to exactly what is due; only which notes can change.
- **First payment wins.** If two desks pay the same purchase, the second is refused: "Already paid by Kojo at 14:32". A repeat of the same payment is harmless.
- **One-person branches:** the person who recorded may pay (setting `payout.sameUserMayPay`, on by default). Switch it off and the paying step needs a different person.
- **Blind check (`payout.blindCheck`, off by default):** the payer must re-enter top, down and price; if the amount differs from the recorded one the payment is stopped and reviewers are notified.
- **Part-paid purchases** (some mobile money or owed, the rest cash): only the cash part waits; the other parts are booked at once as before.
- **Receipt** says "Amount to be paid to you / Not paid yet. Take this receipt to the paying desk" until it is paid.
- **Daily account:** unpaid cash shows under "owed to customers", so the closing balance stays right during the day.

## Offline behaviour (what works, what waits)

- Recording offline works as before. Paying offline works from the list last loaded and from purchases saved on this device; the payout is queued and sent in order.
- A purchase saved on another device while _you_ are offline cannot appear in your list until one of you reaches the server. Search shows nothing for it. This is deliberate: paying something the server has never seen would be a guess.
- If the recorder's device is offline and yours is online, the list fills as soon as their device syncs (it refreshes live and every 30 seconds).
- Not built yet: scanning the receipt's QR code to pay a purchase that has not reached the server. Tell me if you need that when both devices can be offline together.

## Company settings (API now, page in the next batch)

`GET /settings` (everyone), `PUT /settings` (needs the new permission `settings:manage`, given to directors). Keys: `payout.twoStep`, `payout.sameUserMayPay`, `payout.blindCheck`, `allowance.perPerson`, `receipt.printer`, `receipt.paper`, `receipt.businessName`, `receipt.tagline`, `receipt.address`, `receipt.phone`, `receipt.footer`. Changes are written to the audit trail.
**Until the settings page exists**, switch two-step on or off with SQL (as the postgres user):

```sql
-- on
insert into gold.tenant_settings (tenant_id, key, value) select id, 'payout.twoStep', 'true'::jsonb from gold.tenants
on conflict (tenant_id, key) do update set value = 'true'::jsonb;
-- off
update gold.tenant_settings set value = 'false'::jsonb where key = 'payout.twoStep';
```

Switch it on at a quiet time, with no purchase waiting in anyone's outbox.

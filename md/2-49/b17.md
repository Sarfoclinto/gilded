# B17 — price feed: what happens when it fails or jumps (walk-through #51)

I walked through the failure and jump cases and found three real gaps. All three are fixed.

1. **A dismissed price came back.** When GoldBod's price looked wrong the system held it for a director. If the director dismissed it, the next look (every few minutes) held and announced the same price again, until the window ended. Now a price is held once: waiting, accepted or dismissed, it is not held or announced again. A different price is still held as before.
2. **A failure was announced once, then never again.** While GoldBod stays unreachable (or its page changes), directors are now told again every 3 hours.
3. **A window that passed in silence.** If a check window (say 10:30–11:30) ends and nothing at all happened in it, directors are now told: "No new GoldBod price was found between 10:30 and 11:30. Branches are still using the last price." Once per window. It shows in the price feed history as "No new price".

And for the teller: **the New transaction page now warns when the price is older than 12 hours** ("The price was set yesterday at 12:28. Check with a manager that it is still today's price before you pay."). Before, the only sign was a flag written on the purchase afterwards. The 12 hours matches the server's own "old price" flag.

**Not changed (deliberately):** a held price still waits for a director (tellers keep the old price meanwhile). If you would rather it auto-expire or escalate, say so.

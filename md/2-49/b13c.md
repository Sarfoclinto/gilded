# B13c: walk-through 2 (two devices editing)

## What I traced
Moves between states (open/close a day, repay a debt, take a case, transfers, collateral) are all locked on the server, so two devices cannot both win: the second gets a clear refusal. Three real gaps were found and fixed:
1. A cash top-up / take-out saved on a device before the day closed, sent after, was refused (`DAY_CLOSED`) and then hidden: the "refused" card only appeared on the Transactions page, labelled as a transaction, with a View link to nothing. The cash had really moved.
2. Two people editing one customer: the second save silently overwrote the first.
3. A refused entry showed nowhere except the Transactions page.

## Backend
- `backend/db/migrations/0033_late_movements.sql` (new, after 0032): a movement for a closed day is recorded in the day that is open now, with the note starting "Made before the day closed:". With no day open it is still refused, with a clearer message.
- `backend/db/migrations/0034_customer_edit_conflict.sql` (new): `update_customer` refuses a save that would overwrite a field someone else changed (409 `EDIT_CONFLICT`). Old-style saves without `expected` work as before.
- `backend/db/tests/027_late_movements.sql`, `028_customer_edit_conflict.sql` (new).
- `backend/src/GoldValuation.Application/People/PeopleService.cs`: `expected` allowed in the customer PATCH body.

## Web (under `web/src/`)
- `features/transactions/pages/TransactionsPage.tsx`: refused cards say what they are (purchase vs top-up / take-out), show amount and note for movements, and movements can be removed after a confirmation.
- `shared/ui/ConnectionBadge.tsx`: a red "N refused" chip in the header, linking to that list.
- `features/customers/CustomerFormModal.tsx`, `features/customers/api.ts`: sends only changed fields plus what the person saw.

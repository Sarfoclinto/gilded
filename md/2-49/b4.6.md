# B4.6 – Core usability

Apply: `dotnet run --project src/GoldValuation.Api -- migrate` (migration 0017). No seed change. Then `dotnet test`; in `web`: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx vitest run`.

## What it does

1. **Calculator → purchase.** After a result, "Save as a purchase" opens New purchase with top, down and price filled in. Nothing is saved until you confirm there. Used while already on New purchase, it adds the values as one more item and keeps what was typed.
2. **Calculators beside any page.** Round button (bottom right). Desktop: the page and the calculators side by side, drag the bar to resize, X to close. Phone/tablet: bottom sheet. Tabs: Calculator and Notes (amount → notes to hand out). It never saves by itself.
3. **Closing the day.** Switch "Continuous (since the beginning)" / "This day only". Day-only shows cash per note (opened with, in, out, net, as packs/bundles/loose), gold bought that day, and each person lent to/repaid with the sum of debts. Closing itself still checks against the continuous books.

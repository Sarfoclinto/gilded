# B49 — Phone tab bar, Cash in/out redesign, modal white frame, Focus design for the calculators

No database change. Web only.

## What changed

- `web/src/layouts/MobileTabBar.tsx`: rounded pill highlight on the active tab, short labels (Calculator / History / Drawer), raised gold New button, soft shadow.
- `web/src/features/drawer/MovementModal.tsx`: three choice cards (Cash in / Cash out / Office allowance), coloured summary strip, coloured confirm button (Add cash / Take cash out / ...). `initialKind` prop selects the start choice.
- `web/src/features/drawer/pages/DrawerPage.tsx`: green "Cash in" and red "Cash out" buttons open the modal on the right choice.
- `web/src/features/drawer/NoteCountInput.tsx`: optional `accent` and `sign` props (total bar colour and +/−).
- `web/src/styles/antd-theme.css` (section 12): antd 6 moved the modal padding/background onto `.ant-modal-container`, so our navy header sat inside a white frame. Fixed for modals that have a header (`:has(> .ant-modal-header)`). Confirm dialogs (no header) are untouched.

- `web/src/features/calculator/CalculatorPage.tsx`: the calculators (the Calculator page and the tools panel) now have the same Classic | Focus switch as New transaction, and share the same saved choice. Focus shows the big Input and Results cards and the on-screen number pad on touch screens (it reuses `FocusItems`). Save as a purchase, payout panel and Clear work in both. The switch is a small version (`compact`) placed inside the first card's header (Focus: right of "Input"; Classic: right of "Weights", beside Clear), so it takes no row of its own. New transaction keeps the full-size switch in its page header. Files: `LayoutSwitch.tsx` (compact option), `FocusItems.tsx` (headerAction slot).

## Checked

tsc clean; web tests 302 pass. The modal CSS was checked in a small test page (titled modal, confirm dialog, phone width): frame gone, confirm unchanged.

## Not tested

The real app screens (tab bar and Cash in/out on a real phone), and every other modal in the app. Please open a few: New transaction pay modal, exchange notes, settings dialogs, a confirm.

1. Phone width: bottom bar looks tidy, labels fit, current tab highlighted, New button works, Menu opens.
2. Drawer > Cash in: opens on Cash in; enter notes; button says "Add cash". Same for Cash out and the allowance link.
3. Calculator page and the tools-panel calculator: flip Classic/Focus; type top, down, price (pad on a touch screen); amount matches Classic; Save as a purchase carries the values; the choice is the same as on New transaction.
4. Open several modals: no white frame around the header, body padding looks even, close X works.

# B20 — New transaction page flexibility

1. **Stay on the page** (personal, My settings > New purchases > After saving a purchase): "Open the purchase" (as before) or "Stay and start a new one" (fresh form, a green line shows what was just saved with a View link). A receipt that opens automatically (printer) still comes first; held-gold purchases always open the purchase.
2. **Notes to pay with** (personal): Prefer 100s / Fewest notes is the starting choice on every purchase (also the calculator payout). Still changeable per purchase.
3. **Description hidden**: every piece is saved as `lump` (constant `DEFAULT_ITEM_LABEL` in NewTransactionPage.tsx; change it to `plate` there if you prefer).
4. **Create newly seen customers** is a company setting `customers.autoCreate` (default on; Company settings > Paying customers). Off: the purchase keeps the name and phone but no customer is added; a customer already known (same id, or same name and phone) is still linked. Loans always create the borrower.
5. **Loan modal**: customer name/phone search was not working because the picker was never given the branch; fixed, and a loan now needs a picked customer or a phone number.

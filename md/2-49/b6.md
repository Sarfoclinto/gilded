Apply:

1. `dotnet run --project src/GoldValuation.Api -- migrate` (migration 0018).
2. **Re-run the seed with the same arguments as before**, then everyone signs out and in. This adds the permission `discrepancies:review` to the manager and director roles.
3. `dotnet test`; in `web`: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx vitest run`.

## What you get (menu group "Review")

- **Differences** (needs `discrepancies:review`): the inbox of cases from openings, closings and transfer receipts. Take a case, add notes, resolve it (explained / found / recovered / corrected entry / written off, with a written reason of at least 10 characters), or reopen it. Every step is kept in the case history. Resolving records the decision only; closing already adjusted the books.
- **Branch timeline** (needs `reports:read`): every movement of one branch, newest first, with the balance after each. Filter by gold, cash, debts, amounts we owe, and by dates; "Show older" loads more.
- **Between branches** (needs `reports:read`): per pair of branches, cash and gold handed over, mobile money paid on behalf, and mobile money that failed and was covered in cash (what the sending branch still has to hand over).

# B13b: customer after closing, and choosing notes by hand

## Backend

- `backend/db/migrations/0032_after_close_session.sql` (new, after 0031): a closed day is never reopened or edited. A customer after closing is served in a second session on the same date, opened with a written reason, carried from the closing books. Reviewers get a notification. Adds `business_days.session_no` and `continuation_reason` (the one-day-per-date rule becomes one per session) and makes `day_state` pick the latest session.
- `backend/db/tests/026_after_close_session.sql` (new).
- `backend/src/GoldValuation.Application/Operations/Commands.cs`: `OpenDayCommand` gets `AfterClose` (optional).

## Web (under `web/src/`)

- `features/drawer/pages/OpenDayPage.tsx`, `features/drawer/schemas.ts`: when today is closed, the page asks for a reason and opens the second session (no recount; you count when you close).
- `features/transactions/pages/NewTransactionPage.tsx`: the closed-day banner now says what to do.
- `features/calculator/manualNotes.ts` + `manualNotes.test.ts` (new), `features/calculator/PayoutPanel.tsx`: "Customer wants other notes" lets the teller choose the notes by hand. Save stays disabled until they add up exactly to the cash to give and the drawer has every note.

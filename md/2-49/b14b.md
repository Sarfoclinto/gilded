# B14b: changing notes after a customer was paid

A customer who was paid comes back: "change my 200 into two 100s". Now it is a proper entry: the notes the customer gives back and the notes handed out must be worth **exactly** the same (mandatory, checked on screen, in the API and in the database), they must be different notes, and the drawer must hold what is handed out. The amount paid does not change; only the notes do. It shows in the drawer's list for the day, in the audit trail ("Changed notes"), and the drawer balance by note stays right. It works offline like any other drawer entry.

Where: Cash drawer > "Change notes", and on a purchase's page > "Change notes" (links the entry to that purchase).

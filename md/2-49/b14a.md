# B14a: the daily account page, and the office allowance

## What it is
The A4 sheet your tellers write for the manager, built from the closed day: debtors (with what changed since the last closing), cash as bundles of 100 notes and loose notes, debts, goods, what is owed to customers, the office allowance, and the closing balance with its change since the previous closed day. Tellers see their own branch; people who can see several branches get "All branches" (added together) and one chip per branch. The date arrows move between days. Print makes a clean sheet.

Closing balance = cash + debts + goods - owed to customers. The office allowance is paid out of the day's cash (as you said), so it is already out of the cash figure; the page shows it as its own line.
Goods show the days since goods were last sent to the main branch. Earlier stock is "brought forward", and the transfers sent earlier stay one tap away ("Goods sent to the main branch"), so nothing is lost.

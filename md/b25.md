# B25 — Offline foundation, part 1: days offline + offline sign-in

## What changes for people
- **Stored copy lasts 7 days** (was 12–24 h): today's price, branches, drawer, customer list. One setting point: `OFFLINE_DAYS` in `shared/offline/policy.ts` (a Director-set value comes in the next slice).
- **Sign in with no connection.** After someone has signed in on a device with a connection, they can sign in there offline for 7 days after their last online contact. The device keeps a salted PBKDF2 hash of the password (600k rounds), never the password. Permissions are those the server last sent.
- **Sign out keeps the stored copy**, so a teller can sign out and back in offline and still have the price and customers. Other screens' data is dropped. A different *company* signing in on the device wipes it.
- **My settings → This device → Forget this device**: removes stored copy + offline sign-ins + signs out (unsent entries stay).
- The app now sends `X-Client-Version` on every request (server will use it for forced-update rules in a later slice).

## Known limits (by design, next slices)
Offline allowance is fixed at 7 days; no Director setting yet. Recent transactions/debts/receipts are still not readable offline. Revoked users keep access on an offline machine until it next syncs.
